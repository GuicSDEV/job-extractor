Mission Computing, L080, is a new division being established to accelerate how MITRE turns technical possibility into mission capability. We're bringing together supercomputing, AI services, secure lab and cloud infrastructure, and modern engineering tooling, all organized as a front-line function that shapes how teams across MITRE build, experiment, and deliver. The AI and Mission Enablement department is the applied AI and services adoption arm of Mission Computing. Its role is to make MITRE's AI and mission platform investments practical for mission work: giving staff usable paths to approved tools, workflows, reusable patterns, data integrations, development environments, hands-on experimentation, and high-touch technical support. The department helps teams move beyond isolated experiments into secure, repeatable ways of using platforms and computing environments in all aspects of their work.

As Mission Computing stands up, AI and Mission Enablement will sit between infrastructure and practice. The department will work with platform teams, mission users, laboratories, Enterprise Technology, Information Security Services, and AI communities across MITRE to standardize how capabilities are discovered, integrated, taught, supported, and scaled across approved data and security environments. Its work is to make AI and platforms easier to learn, easier to trust, easier to connect to MITRE workflows, and easier to apply responsibly to mission problems. AI Enablement is the group that turns platforms, APIs, agents, data workflows, secure compute, and developer experiences into a working foundation for a mission-centric MITRE.

Roles & Responsibilities:

MITRE's AI and Mission Enablement Department within the Mission Computing Division is seeking an AI Solutions Architect Intern who is currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree. Working with a technical mentor and more senior staff, the intern will gain hands-on experience helping mission teams use approved AI tools, APIs, data workflows, and development environments. The intern will contribute to a scoped project through guided experimentation, implementation, testing, and documentation while learning how usable, secure, and responsible AI solutions are developed. Roles and responsibilities include:

Participate in discussions with technical and mission users alongside a mentor; ask questions, confirm understanding, and help document workflow needs, user questions, and project objectives. 
Build or adapt small prototype components, scripts, prompt examples, or API integrations using approved AI tools, sample code, and development environments under the direction of more senior staff. 
Learn and follow established security, data-handling, and responsible-AI practices; use approved tools and data, recognize the limitations of AI outputs, and seek guidance before taking unfamiliar actions. 
Run defined tests and experiments, compare outputs against mentor-provided criteria, and document results and limitations; use existing guidance to troubleshoot basic issues and ask for help when needed. 
Contribute to starter kits, reusable examples, setup instructions, and project documentation so teammates can understand and reproduce the intern's work; incorporate feedback from technical reviews. 
Assist with demonstrations, tutorials, hands-on learning activities, and routine user-support tasks alongside experienced staff; capture questions and feedback for the team to review. 
Work with a mentor to set learning goals and complete a defined project; communicate progress, apply feedback, collaborate with teammates, and present the project's results and lessons learned at the end of the internship.
